## Description (4000)
KindDose is a gentle medicine reminder made for everyone, especially anyone who finds other apps too small, too busy, or too complicated.

ONE BIG BUTTON
When it's time, KindDose shows your next medicine with its photo, name, and dose, and one large "I took it" button. Logging a dose takes seconds. You can also tap "I took it" right on the reminder.

A SMILE EVERY TIME
After each dose, KindDose says something kind and shares a gentle joke. Taking care of yourself should feel good.

NO GUILT
Missed one? That's okay. KindDose lets you mark it as taken late or skipped, and offers a calm one-minute breathing exercise. No red crosses, no broken streaks.

EASY TO ADD MEDICINES
• Scan a pharmacy label or packet with your camera
• Say it out loud: "Metformin, 500 milligrams, morning and night"
• Type it in, one simple step at a time
• Import from Apple Health on supported iPhones
You always see an "Is this correct?" screen before anything is saved.

BUILT FOR ACCESSIBILITY
• Large text up to the biggest iPhone sizes
• Full VoiceOver support
• Big buttons that are easy to tap
• Works with Voice Control and Switch Control
• Respects Reduce Motion and Increase Contrast
• Calm backgrounds that change with the time of day

REMINDERS THAT REACH YOU
• Time-sensitive reminders with "I took it" and "Remind me in 10 minutes"
• Gentle follow-up reminders if you haven't logged a dose
• On newer iPhones, an optional alarm-style sound that rings even in Silent mode
• Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets
• Siri: "I took my medicine in KindDose"

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No accounts. No ads. No tracking. Everything stays on your iPhone.

KindDose is a reminder tool, not medical advice. Always follow your doctor's or pharmacist's instructions.

## App Review notes
KindDose is a medication reminder and logging tool. No account or sign-in. All data is stored on the device (SwiftData); the app makes no network calls.

- HealthKit: optional "Import from Apple Health" when adding a medicine (My medicines > Add > Apple Health). It requests READ access to the user's medications only (HKUserAnnotatedMedication) to pre-fill the add-medicine form; nothing is written to Health and nothing leaves the device. The option is hidden where unsupported.
- AlarmKit (iOS 26+): optional setting in Settings, "Alarm sound", so medicine reminders can ring in Silent mode. Off by default and requested only when the user turns it on.
- Time-sensitive notifications are used only for scheduled medicine reminders.
- Camera / microphone / speech: optional ways to add a medicine (scan a label, or speak it). Results are always shown on a confirmation screen before saving.
- The app gives no medical advice; for missed doses it tells users to check the leaflet or ask their pharmacist.

To test quickly: complete the 1-minute setup, tap My medicines > Add > Type, add a medicine with a time a few minutes ahead, then return to Home and tap "I took it".
